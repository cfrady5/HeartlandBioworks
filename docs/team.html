Executive Board

The Heartland BioWorks Executive Board is comprised of key Indiana stakeholders who provide expert oversight of hub activities and guide progress. Governance of the hub is supported by two advisory boards. The Scientific Advisory Board provides expertise on technical direction, BioLaunch fund deployment, and positions the hub to leverage emerging trends in the sector. The Community Impact Advisory Board is comprised of local non-profit leaders who help ensure hub programs are responsive to community needs.
