Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about SciTechCONNECT and how it connects campus research to Department of War missions.

What is SciTechCONNECT?
SciTechCONNECT is the central hub that helps HBCUs, Tribal Colleges, and universities translate academic research into Department of War (DoW) science and technology capabilities — connecting people, programs, and funding in one place.
Who is SciTechCONNECT for?
Faculty, student researchers, technology-transfer offices, and innovation leaders at HBCUs, Tribal Colleges, and universities who want to engage with the Department of War S&T ecosystem.
What is an Innovation Profile?
Your Innovation Profile captures your research focus, capabilities, and goals. It helps us match you with relevant DoW opportunities, program managers, and collaborators across the network.
Does it cost anything to join?
No. Building your Innovation Profile and accessing SciTechCONNECT resources, webinars, and the community is free for eligible institutions and their researchers.
What does the Learn–Bridge–Fund–Deploy journey mean?
It is our roadmap from campus lab to national impact: Learn the landscape, Bridge to partners and program managers, Fund your work through DoW pathways, and Deploy research into fielded capability. You can enter the journey at whichever stage fits you.
How does SciTechCONNECT help with funding?
We help you navigate Department of War funding pathways — including SBIR/STTR, Broad Agency Announcements, and grants — and connect you with the right program offices. Specific opportunities and eligibility vary, so reach out for current guidance.
Do I need prior defense experience to participate?
Not at all. SciTechCONNECT is designed to onboard researchers who are new to the Department of War ecosystem, with curriculum, webinars, and one-on-one guidance to get you oriented.
How do I connect with program managers and primes?
The Bridge stage of the journey introduces you to DoW program managers, prime contractors, and peer institutions through curated events, matchmaking, and the SciTechCONNECT network.
Where can I find upcoming events and webinars?
Visit the Upcoming Events & Webinars page from the hero buttons above. New sessions on funding, partnerships, and capability transition are added regularly.
How do I get started?
Select Build Your Innovation Profile at the top of the page or in the Join the Mission section below. It takes only a few minutes and unlocks tailored access to the ecosystem.
